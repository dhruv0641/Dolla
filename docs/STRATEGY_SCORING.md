# Strategy and idea scoring

`flowx_os.agents.strategy` scores caller-supplied 0–100 ratings with a versioned weight profile. The default weights are originality 15%, audience fit 25%, evidence strength 25%, production feasibility 15%, and brand alignment 20%. Every result includes each dimension's input rating, weight, and weighted contribution; the total is the rounded sum, bounded from 0 to 100.

Ratings are explicit inputs from a strategy component or reviewer; this module does not generate ratings or infer evidence quality. An evidence-strength rating above zero requires at least one evidence reference. The default profile requires one source for `READY_FOR_REVIEW`; candidates with no sources remain `NEEDS_EVIDENCE` regardless of their numeric score under that default. A profile may explicitly set `minimum_evidence_sources=0` for workflows that intentionally score unverified concepts, in which case a high-scoring, otherwise unblocked candidate may be `READY_FOR_REVIEW` without citations. Typed blockers such as rights review, fact checking, policy review, or brand conflict force `BLOCKED`. Ideas below the configured threshold are `BELOW_THRESHOLD`.

Ranking is deterministic: ready ideas first, then needs-evidence, below-threshold, and blocked; within each status, score descends, then title and stable UUID break ties. `READY_FOR_REVIEW` does not mean approved or publishable; human/editorial and policy gates remain required. No model provider, persistence, experiment tuning, or live integration is included. Tests use fixed inputs and verify the score breakdown, blockers, evidence state, thresholds, and tie behavior.
